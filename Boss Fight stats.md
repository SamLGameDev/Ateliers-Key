# Boss Fight stats

### Positional statistics for height of floor and ceiling

#### Height range to activate movement:

Vert: 

* 900 - hand clips floor slightly 
* 800 - Hand still clips floor, if player is close its unnoticeable, however if they move then it is possible to see, good height for the 1st level
* 700 - Still clipping floor, same as 800; slightly worse for the 1dst floor as you begin to see more of the plate so could cause clipping issues/collision when falling off from key
* 600 - No longer clipping, however the plate is same level as player so could feel awkward when falling to bottom level to unlock the plates ---- dependent on how far away the player is and reach of hands on boss



800 - 600



Hand Clip Point:

* 620 - not noticeable
* 630 - even less noticeable
* 640 - less noticeable
* 650 - almost unnoticeable clipping
* 655 - too noticeable
* 660 - less noticeable as the point of clipping is at the fastest movement of the anim
* 670 - clipping more noticeable almost similar too 700



##### **620**



#### Length range to activate movement:



Horiz:

At 600 level

* 100  - Palm swipe is almost at a perfect pos but again this close could cause issues when the plate falls off,
* 200  - floor clips into boss
* 0    - Decent distance boss can still hit player however plate issue mentioned above applies to such
* -100 - Boss can hit player but will be more of a fingertip than palm dependent on player pos
* -200 - Boss misses player completely 



At 700 level

* 100  - hand clip is incredibly noticeable due to closeness of platform, again collision could become an issue here
* 0    - Hand clip is more noticeable when far away, but pos can still allow for it to hit player
* -100 - hand clip is less noticeable as the hand doesn't reach full length of the platform
* -200 - hand misses platform entirely



At 800 level

* 100  - again noticeable clipping even when close to boss
* 0    - good height and placement feels good, however hand clip is noticeable to the player at any point, prevents clipping issue with the plates
* -100 - hand misses majority of the platform clips the first 5%
* -200 - Misses completely
